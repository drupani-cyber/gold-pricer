Why does "Cost" show dollars but say Included?

The cost column shows the on-demand value of your usage. If it says Included, your plan covered it and you were charged $0.
  • Value (on-demand rate) is what it would cost without a plan.
  • Included means your plan covered that usage for this period.
  • You only pay extra when you exceed your included allowance.
